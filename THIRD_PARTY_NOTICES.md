## SVG Motion Cookbook

The heading SVG assets (`assets/heading-*.svg`) and their generator
(`tools/build_profile_motion.py`) adapt the clip-path reveal from pattern 03 and
color interpolation from pattern 06 of **SVG Motion Cookbook**, by Water
(WaterTian). These adaptations use a single finite underline reveal and shimmer,
stationary text, theme-aware colors, and explicit reduced-motion fallbacks.
